Timeline

13 NOV · ~20:00 UTC

First ATC frequency problems reported at Chicago O’Hare, then JFK. Chicago O’Hare/Midway ground stops follow.

13 NOV · 20:54 UTC

Nationwide ground stop issued for all departures. All 22 ARTCCs affected. Advisory 120: “COMMUNICATIONS FAILURE.”

13 NOV · ~21:00–23:00 UTC

Radars and radio frequencies reportedly failed together. Backup communications fail with primary. Hawaii DOT reports ~80 inbound and taxiway/runway parking.

13 NOV · ~23:00 UTC

WSJ: L3Harris network at center; handles radio frequencies. CBS: update to private communications network caused reboot/crash.

14 NOV · 00:40 UTC

Cirium: 496 flights diverted to origin; 104 to different airport. DCA/FLL closed to diversions; BDL closed. No fuel emergencies reported.

14 NOV · 00:54 UTC

FAA NAS status: ground stop remains, reason “EQUIPMENT / OUTAGE.” FlightAware: 10,000+ delays, 4,000 cancellations. Hawaii and Alaska confirmed exempt from the ground stop; West Coast–Hawaii flights resumed, then were halted by SpaceX debris.

14 NOV · 01:06 UTC

CNN: faulty network routers, starting at Chicago Midway, cascaded nationwide. FlightAware: 10,343 delays / 1,479 cancellations (Guardian); ABC/GMA report 4,000+ cancellations. Root cause still unconfirmed.

14 NOV · 01:12 UTC

FAA NAS summary still shows national ground stop ALL / EQUIPMENT / OUTAGE, but DCA, FLL, and BDL no longer appear in the active airport-closure table. BOS/LGA/SAN/SFO ground delays remain.

14 NOV · 01:18 UTC

Correction: FAA NAS list still shows DCA, FLL, and BDL as active closures (DCA until 11:00 UTC; FLL until 09:00 UTC; BDL until 05:00 UTC). The earlier empty closure table was a summary-page discrepancy. National ground stop remains.

14 NOV · 01:24 UTC

FAA NAS summary at 01:24 UTC shows no active ground stops and no national programs; the national ground stop appears lifted. Advisory 111 still lists the original event window. DCA/FLL/BDL closures and BOS/LGA/SAN/SFO ground delays remain.

14 NOV · 01:54 UTC

Washington Post confirms the ground stop was lifted shortly after 8:00 pm ET. Cirium: 827 diversions (670 return, 157 alternate); 1.5M passengers affected. DCA/FLL/BDL closures and BOS/LGA/SAN/SFO ground delays remain.

14 NOV · 02:00 UTC

WIRED: Duffy blames “old switches, old routers” and a “data overload”; Mullin says “new technology was overwhelming the older system” and “putting patches into the system.” FTI/L3Harris named. FlightAware: 6,300 cancellations, 9,500 delays. Root cause still not formally confirmed.

What the evidence supports

The strongest current fit is a common-mode FTI transport failure — core router/route policy, pseudowire/TDM gateway, or shared timing/audio path — not an application software update and not a voice-switch-only failure.
